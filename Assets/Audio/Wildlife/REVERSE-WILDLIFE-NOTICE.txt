Deer calls remain original procedural sound design for Racer.
Coyote calls now use the National Park Service field recording "Coyotes",
Mojave National Preserve, California. Credit: NPS. Public domain; no required
attribution. This voluntary credit and source record are retained in the game.
Source page: https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/sounds-coyotes.htm
Recording: https://www.nps.gov/nps-audiovideo/legacy/mp3/nri/avElement/nri-CoyotesMOJA.mp3
Rights: https://www.nps.gov/subjects/sound/gallery.htm states the files are public
domain and may be downloaded. Verified 2026-09-28. No copyright symbol or
third-party credit on this recording. Two nonoverlapping three-second excerpts,
mono PCM, gain adjusted and boundary fades; original pitch and natural calls.
Reproduce with Tools/Prepare-CoyoteRecording.cs; offsets and processing are in
Docs/FiveUpdates/coyote-mastering.txt. Existing Coyote-original-* asset IDs are
retained for scene compatibility; the content is recorded coyotes, not synthesis.
Other wildlife recordings and their LICENSE.txt remain unchanged.

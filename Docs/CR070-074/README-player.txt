RACER 0.12.0-review1 - CR-070 through CR-074
Single-player Windows review build. New work awaits Dan's review.

Choose Track from the ready menu:
- Street Loop: all four vehicles.
- Forest Loop: motorcycle and ATV.
- Street Loop Reverse: all four vehicles; Laurel Switchbacks and Granite Creek Cut.
- Forest Loop Reverse: motorcycle and ATV; Fern Gully and Granite Saddle.

Reverse variants have separate starts, gates, route direction and record categories.
Optional branches earn only their listed bypass gates; missed unrelated gates still cost five seconds.
R on keyboard / Y on Xbox-style controller recovers locally while retaining progress.
Wrong Way appears after about five seconds of meaningful wrong-direction travel, with a local direction arrow and the active reset binding. Pause freezes the timer.

Driver faces/hair and occasional deer/coyotes are new. Animals are ambient and have no vehicle collision.
Radio channels and staged music are retained. See RADIO.md for custom collections.
Launch Play-Racer.cmd in the project, or Racer.exe here. No external upload has been made.

See Docs/CR070-074/VALIDATION.md in the project for actual test results and limitations.

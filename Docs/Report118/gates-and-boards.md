# 0.118 Part B: Leviathan's turn boards and gates

Arrow boards: all removed (0.116 / 0.117, race only). Chevrons: kept only at a corner between two real roads (Street Loop streets, Hwy 92, the mountain roads CR110 / CR117, Climbing / Downhill Ridge Cut). Every other turn (off a road onto a trail, the lake path, Kyle's driveway or the link up the bank) lost its arrow and chevrons and has a gate in or just past it. Gates were added so that from every gate the next one is in view as you leave it (Report118Gates: line of sight from the driver's eye 8 m past the gate, within 250 m and 50 degrees of ahead; pairs with a flight between are not checked). Full pair-by-pair lists: [Probe/gates118-LeviathanForward.txt](Probe/gates118-LeviathanForward.txt), [Probe/gates118-LeviathanReverse.txt](Probe/gates118-LeviathanReverse.txt).

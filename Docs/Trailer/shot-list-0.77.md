# Woodstock Rush — trailer shot list

A 40 to 45 second trailer built from 15 short clips. Updated 2026-10-04 for the 0.77 build (Trailer Mode, camera views, dedicated Free Roam world). Copy of the claude.ai Project doc `claude/trailer-shot-list.md`, placed here 2026-10-08 for the 0.97 round (Part C), which updates it for the current game.

## Before filming

- OBS: NVENC encoder at a high quality setting, 60 fps. 1440p is easier on a GTX 1660 Ti while recording; 4K if it holds 60.
- Turn the radio off so music can be added in the edit. Keep engine, rain and thunder.
- In the garage, pick one rider look and one colour per vehicle and keep them for every shot.
- Start Free Roam, press **F8** for Trailer Mode. Useful keys: cameras **1–9**, **Tab** camera action, **Z** hold for slow motion, **T** time of day, **B** weather, **O** moon phase, **L** lightning now, **K** pause the clock, **P** screenshot.
- Record each shot as its own clip, 10 to 20 seconds, with a couple of seconds either side. Three takes of anything with a jump.
- Name clips by shot number: `01-dawn-road`, `02-rider`, and so on.

## Notes on shot 15 (matching the cover art)

- The art shows two vehicles in the air at once. Free Roam has only the player, so film this as a race on a course that runs over that crest, with Trailer Mode on, an AI field of ATVs, and the player in the blue car. Let the pack catch up so both leave the ground together.
- Plant the Fixed camera first (5, then Tab to re-plant where you want it), low and close to the road, then drive back over the crest toward it. Hold Z for slow motion as you take off.
- Also press P at the peak for a full-resolution still. That still, with the logo over it, could become a store or thumbnail image made from the real game.
- If the crest does not launch the car enough at racing speed, that is worth reporting: a small bump there could be added so the cover moment exists in the game.

## Structure

- 0 to 10 s: calm opening (1), then the garage (2, 3).
- 10 to 20 s: racing and the neighbourhood (4 to 7).
- 20 to 33 s: the world in different light and weather (8 to 13).
- 33 to 45 s: the giant jump (14), the cover shot (15), the title.

## Tips

- Ride each shot longer than needed; the edit keeps the best 2 to 4 seconds.
- Try camera 8 (Auto) on a long ride and keep recording. It will find angles not listed here.
- If a rival or traffic car blocks a shot, redo it.
- Not available in Free Roam: the Forest cave and the Mountain race roads. To show those, film them in their races with Trailer Mode on.

## Music

Use a track you have the right to use (royalty-free, or your own). Something that starts calm, lifts at about 10 seconds and peaks around 33 seconds fits this structure.

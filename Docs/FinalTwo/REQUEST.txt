==================================================

Desired:

BRIEF
LOCALIZED
ATMOSPHERIC.

Not:

CONSTANT
SHRILL
REPETITIVE
DOMINATING.

A short cluster of squeaks/scurrying as the rats flee is appropriate.

Do not continuously loop rat sounds throughout the tunnel.

==================================================
PREFERRED OBSTACLE:
PARTIAL CAVE-IN / ROCKFALL
==================================================

Create one believable partial cave-in somewhere in the cave where the existing
geometry provides an appropriate location.

The cave-in should block MOST of the easy straight racing line.

Use:

- fallen rock;
- broken stone;
- rubble;
- natural cave debris.

Leave a deliberately usable passage around/through it.

==================================================
DRIVING CHALLENGE
==================================================

The obstacle should require the player to:

SEE IT
->
CHOOSE / LINE UP THE OPENING
->
ADJUST THEIR LINE
->
PASS THROUGH CLEANLY
->
ACCELERATE AWAY.

A skilled player who anticipates the opening should be able to maintain useful
speed.

A careless player who simply drives straight ahead should:

- hit rock;
- lose meaningful speed;
- need to correct their line.

==================================================
DIFFICULT BUT FAIR
==================================================

Make this one of the more difficult moments in the cave.

However, do NOT make it:

- pixel-perfect;
- barely wider than a motorcycle;
- impossible for an ATV;
- dependent on crawling through;
- an unavoidable vehicle-flipping trap.

Both:

MOTORCYCLE
and
ATV

must have a legitimate racing line through it.

The ATV should have less margin for error, which is acceptable.

==================================================
NO INVISIBLE CHEATING
==================================================

The intended opening must be visually obvious enough to understand at racing
speed.

Do not use:

- invisible walls;
- unexplained colliders;
- automatic reset volumes.

The physical rockfall itself should create the challenge.

==================================================
NO EASY SECOND PATH
==================================================

Do not leave a huge open gap on the opposite side that completely defeats the
obstacle.

There should be ONE obvious intended fast passage.

Other accidental ways around the rockfall should be:

- slower;
- awkward;
- or naturally blocked by cave geometry.

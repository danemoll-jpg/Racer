RACER 0.4.0-review1 - Windows x64 single-player review
Extract the complete ZIP. Run Racer.exe. No installation or Editor required.
Ready: Garage selects Street Classic, Longroof GT, Needle 600 motorcycle or Trail Four ATV.
Preview and descriptions explain speed, response, stability and collision strength.
Selection persists and applies before racing. Use Done / ready, then Start race.
Ready/results: choose Race vs 3 AI or Solo/time trial; Traffic On/Off; Easy/Normal/Hard.
Three laps by default. Three car opponents, four two-way traffic cars.
Cars use the selected car profile. With motorcycle/ATV, AI use slower Street Classics.
W/Up or RT: accelerate. S/Down or LT: brake; keep held through stop to reverse.
A/D/arrows or left stick: steer. Reverse steering follows direction of travel.
R/Y: reset vehicle, abandoning current lap; race clock and penalties remain.
After a wipeout, regain control or use R/Y. Wait if the reset pad is occupied.
Enter/Escape/Start: pause/resume. D-pad/stick/arrows: menu selection. A/Space: confirm.
Mouse buttons work throughout. B/Escape returns from Garage/settings.
Master, ambience, Race/UI and vehicle volume remain in Settings.
Checkpoint misses continue racing: 5 seconds plus a distance charge for major cuts.
Finish ranking uses adjusted times; wait up to 90 seconds for remaining finishers.
Records are separated by stable vehicle, rules, mode, difficulty, traffic and lap count.
Legacy records/settings remain on disk. No multiplayer, online services or paid assets.
See VERSION.txt for the exact source commit. No upload or distribution performed.

MAP AND EXPLORATION
M / controller View opens the map while driving or paused. It is also available
under Exploration / clean-lap ghosts. Mouse wheel zooms; drag pans; click places
a waypoint or selects a discovered landmark. WASD / left stick pans; triggers
zoom; A / Space places a waypoint at the center; left/right D-pad cycles travel
destinations; X travels. B / Esc / M closes the map.

Fast travel works only in FREE ROAM, only to discovered destinations, and only
when the arrival footprint has supported ground and vehicle clearance. Traffic
can temporarily block arrival. Travel cancels active attempts. It does not
reveal the intervening map, award laps or activities, or collect items en route.
Visited terrain is NOT a promise that you have searched it thoroughly.

ACORN HUNT
All 24 IDs retain existing progress. Previously found IDs stay collected even
though their locations moved. To hunt the new placement: Exploration /
clean-lap ghosts > Restart relocated acorn hunt... > CONFIRM: restart ONLY
the 24 acorns. Back cancels. This preserves map discovery, records, ghosts,
settings and music choices. Look for small three-stone cairns leading away
from ordinary roads and trails. Regional counts help narrow the search.

SUMMIT AND CAMPSITE
Follow Kyle's wooded driveway to the lake and the existing mountain trails.
The permanent campsite is just south of the summit ridge, near world X=989,
Z=75. Two seated guys and a campfire sit a few feet from a round pop-up tent.
The summit launch approach curls to the east/north of the summit, then heads
WEST toward the neighborhood: approach around (1070,190), lip (970,190).
Follow the high-risk approach sign and the broad supported landing. The marked
return loops north via (805,250), then joins the existing ridge trail at
(901,154), at a matching elevation. Follow the existing trail downhill.
Do not drive back up the launch face. There is no mountain race in this pass.
Known edge limit: far southern motorcycle and fast ATV approaches can roll
or land too hard to score, depending on the variant. Use the center
or normal offsets, brake after landing, and use R / Y for supported recovery.
The actual edge failures remain in the validation report.

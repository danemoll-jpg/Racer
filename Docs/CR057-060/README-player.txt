Racer 0.9.0-review1 - local Windows review build

Extract the entire ZIP, then open Racer.exe. In the project, Play-Racer.cmd opens Builds/Latest/Racer.exe.

Track selects Street Loop or Forest Loop. Forest Loop retains its six main jumps, optional cave, narrow trails and motorcycle/ATV roster. Street Loop retains all four vehicles. Garage and paint choices are unchanged.

This review adds shallow driveable water, occasional AI judgment errors, cave-entry bats and varied neighborhood life. Water slows immersed vehicles; steer, stop or reverse to drive out. Airborne crossings remain dry. Bats and people are ambient and cannot collide with vehicles. Household occupancy changes between races and remains stable through pause and local recovery. Floating world labels are removed; gates and screen HUD remain.

RT / W accelerates; LT / S brakes and reverses; left stick / A-D steers; Y / R recovers locally. Start / Enter / Escape pauses. Menus use D-pad/arrows, A/Space to confirm, B to return.

Radio: D-pad Down / M cycles channels and Off. Left / [ selects the previous song; Right / ] the next; Up / I shows channel and artist/title. Settings > Music offers independent volume, source and rescan controls. See RADIO.md. Only songs deliberately staged in BundleMusic are packaged.

True missed gates retain the flat five-second penalty. Authorized shortcuts and recovery preserve earned progress. New comparable records use street-v9-life and lake-v3-shallows; previous boards and saves remain available.

Awaiting Dan's review. Scripted driving, rendered captures and audio-state checks do not establish human driving feel, subjective sound quality or physical-controller acceptance. Art is simple procedural low-poly work with modest joint animation and synthetic water/bat sounds. Stunt challenges and ghosts are not included. No upload or online service.

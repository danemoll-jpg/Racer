RACER 0.3.0-review1 - Windows x64 single-player review
Extract the complete ZIP. Run Racer.exe. No installation or Editor required.
Ready/results menus: choose Race vs 3 AI or Solo/time trial; choose Traffic On/Off.
Default race: three laps. Three named AI opponents; four two-way traffic cars.
W/Up or RT: accelerate. S/Down or LT: brake/reverse. A/D/arrows or left stick: steer.
R or Y: fixed-grid car reset, abandons current lap; race clock and penalties remain.
Enter/Escape/Start: pause/resume. D-pad/arrows: menu selection. A/Space: confirm.
Master, ambience, Race/UI and vehicle volume remain in Settings.
Checkpoint misses continue the race: 5 seconds plus a distance-based charge for major cuts.
HUD separates driving time and penalties. Track position is not final adjusted-time ranking.
Finish results wait at most 90 seconds after first finisher; unfinished cars then DNF.
Overall race timeout: 20 minutes. No multiplayer or online services.
Records have new speed/rules/mode/traffic categories; previous records are preserved.
Tuning is provisional: 49 m/s forward target, 14.5 m/s2 acceleration; Dan reviews handling.
See VERSION.txt for the exact source commit. No upload or distribution performed.

Woodstock Rush 0.25.0-review1

Street Loop Reverse / Laurel Pass: replaces the malformed elevated shortcut
with a supported local route. Its final approach and ramp are straight and
launch directly toward the main road. No artificial boost or vehicle tuning.

Mountain Loop Reverse: localized first-arrow road surface correction only.
The arrow remains visual-only. No larger Mountain route redesign.

Recovery: R / Y queues a short 0.8-second reset delay. Recent occupied road
positions advance after stable grounded forward driving. Ramps and airborne
positions are excluded. A completed landing retires pre-jump fallback history.
No additional checkpoint, lap or forward progress is granted by recovery.

Targeted technical checks only. Detailed driving acceptance remains Dan's
gameplay test; no full races were repeatedly playtested for this release.

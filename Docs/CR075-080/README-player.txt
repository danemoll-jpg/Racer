Racer 0.13.0 review / route fixes + arcade challenges
Awaiting Dan's review. No external upload.

Play-Racer.cmd starts the complete Builds/Latest runtime.
Track selects Street, Forest, Street Reverse or Forest Reverse.
Forest remains motorcycle/ATV only.

Choose Free Roam in the main menu to explore without countdown, race opponents,
gates, laps, finish, penalties or wrong-way warnings. Existing traffic and world
systems remain. Esc / Start pauses; choose Return to menu to select a race.
R / Y resets to recent clear supported road/trail. Race resets retain earned progress.

Free Roam pause menu: cycle Activity, then Start / retry selected activity.
Jump: take off in the named jump area and land upright and stably. Horizontal
takeoff-to-landing distance and airborne time produce feedback points:
10 points/metre + 100 points/second, rounded. Jump medals use distance in metres.
Reset, teleport, spawn drop, short jitter, wipeout, wet or hard landings do not award
a successful jump. Points never change race time, penalties, gates or vehicle power.

Fence line smash: distinct designated breakable fence sections count per attempt;
bronze/silver/gold are 3/6/10 within 8 seconds. People, animals, debris and unrelated
props are not targets. Deliberate retry clears the set and restores props safely.
Pause freezes the attempt. Reset cancels it; use the pause menu to retry.

Two speed traps per course operate automatically in Free Roam, in both directions.
They report actual crossing speed in km/h, best and medal. Leave the area by at least
25 metres to rearm; lingering, spawn/reset and teleport crossings do not count.
Activity bests/medals are saved separately by activity, course/rules and vehicle.

Driving/radio/garage/color/controller controls remain the existing ones.
Music: D-pad Down / M cycles channel and Off; Left/Right or [ / ] changes song;
Up / I shows title. Stage music in BundleMusic and use Package-Racer.cmd to package.

Read Docs/CR075-080/VALIDATION.md for measured results and remaining limitations.
Automated tests do not establish human driving enjoyment, physical controller
acceptance or speaker-level listening. CR-081 remains approved for the following pass.

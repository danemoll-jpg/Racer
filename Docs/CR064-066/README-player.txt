Racer 0.10.0-review1 - local Windows review build

Extract the entire ZIP, then open Racer.exe. In the project, Play-Racer.cmd opens Builds/Latest/Racer.exe.

Settings > Estimate AI at your finish: On finalizes unfinished AI when you finish.
The default is Off, preserving normal AI simulation. After finishing with it Off,
open Pause and choose Skip waiting / estimate remaining AI, or Resume to keep watching.
Projected classifications are labeled ~ and Estimated. Existing finishes, DNFs, penalties
and your measured result remain intact. Projections cannot enter measured record boards.
Estimates use route/lap progress and recent moving pace, with course/vehicle/difficulty
fallbacks. They are approximate predictions, not simulated future races.

Both cars, the motorcycle and ATV have revised original stylized models, alloy wheels,
lights, distinct trim, cabin occupants or articulated seated riders, and simple steering/lean.
Physics, handling and eligibility remain unchanged. Appearance awaits Dan's review.

Changing channel shows the folder name followed by Radio. Off reads Radio Off;
root songs use General Radio. Song information reads Artist: ... and Song: ... on two lines.
Long metadata is truncated with an ellipsis. Missing metadata uses Unknown Artist and filename.

Both courses retain physical lettering and the exact cave warning, wildlife/audio,
neighborhood people, ambient street traffic, water, AI mistakes, local recovery and top-ten boards.
Street Loop offers all four vehicles; Forest Loop offers motorcycle and ATV.

RT / W accelerates; LT / S brakes and reverses; left stick / A-D steers; Y / R recovers locally.
Start / Enter / Escape pauses. D-pad/arrows navigate menus; A/Space confirms; B returns.
Radio: D-pad Down / M cycles channels and Off. Left / [ previous song; Right / ] next;
Up / I shows artist and song. Settings > Music controls source, volume and rescan.
Only songs deliberately staged in BundleMusic are packaged. See RADIO.md and Licenses.

Awaiting Dan's review. Automated tests and rendered captures do not establish subjective art,
speaker/headphone listening, human driving feel or physical-controller acceptance.
No split screen, networking, stunt challenges or ghost mode. No external upload.

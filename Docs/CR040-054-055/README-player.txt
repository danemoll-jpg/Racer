Racer 0.7.0-review1 — local Windows review build

Extract the complete ZIP, then open Racer.exe. In the project, Play-Racer.cmd opens the complete Builds/Latest runtime.

Main menu > Track selects Street Loop or Lake & Woods. Main menu > Records / Top 10 opens Lap and Race boards. Select a saved category, or the current track/vehicle/race configuration. Results also provides record-board access.

Driving: RT / W accelerates; LT / S brakes and reverses; left stick / A-D steers; Y / R recovers locally. Start / Enter / Escape pauses. Menus: D-pad or arrows navigate; A / Space confirms; B returns. Garage chooses vehicles and colors. Opponent vehicles chooses the resolved roster. Difficulty and handling are unchanged.

Radio: D-pad Down / M cycles channels and Off. Left / [ is previous song; Right / ] next song; Up / I shows channel and artist/title. These D-pad actions apply during gameplay only. Settings > Music offers the same controls and independent volume/source/rescan options. See RADIO.md for folder-named channels and portable Music setup.

Each genuine missed required gate costs exactly five seconds. Marked authorized shortcuts grant their explicit bypass gates without penalties. Recovery preserves current progress. Race time begins at GO; laps begin on crossing START. Total-race records require a completed race; completed eligible laps can remain after quitting.

Records and preferences are local to the Windows user. Track/course version, vehicle, mode/difficulty, roster and traffic separate comparable boards. Total races also separate lap count. Historical best files are preserved. Migrated entries with no known date say Unknown (legacy).

This build awaits Dan's review. Automated driving, virtual input and decoded audio tests are not human driving, physical-controller testing or listening. No online multiplayer, uploads or online leaderboard.

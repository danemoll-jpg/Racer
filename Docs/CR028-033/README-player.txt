RACER 0.5.0-review1 - Windows x64 single-player review
Awaiting Dan's approval. Extract the complete ZIP and run Racer.exe.

Ready: choose Solo or Race vs 3 AI, traffic On/Off and Easy/Normal/Hard.
Garage: Street Classic, Longroof GT, Needle 600 motorcycle, Trail Four ATV.
Six body-color swatches preview immediately and persist separately for each vehicle.
Opponent vehicles: select each real vehicle profile, Random, or Mixed.
Resolved opponents are shown before GO and remain fixed for restart/rematch.
Use Reroll Random / Mixed deliberately to change random selections.

W/Up or RT: accelerate. S/Down or LT: brake; hold through stop to reverse.
A/D/arrows or left stick: steer. R/Y: recover locally, keeping time/laps/penalties.
Recovery rights the vehicle on clear support or finds nearby support behind it.
If no safe local support exists, it waits and retries; it never silently sends you to START.
Enter/Escape/Start: pause/resume. Pause has separate Restart Race, Quit Race and Quit Game.
Quit Race / Return to Menu abandons this event and allows another vehicle/race.
Restart Race starts the whole event over and restores breakable props.
D-pad/stick/arrows: menu selection. A/Space: confirm. Mouse works throughout.
B/Escape: back from garage/settings/opponent selection.

HUD shows km/h, elapsed lap/race stopwatch, lap fraction and position fraction.
Missed gates still cost 5 seconds plus a distance charge for substantial cuts.
Short penalty notices explain charges; Pause/Results show adjusted timing and details.
R/Y itself adds no penalty and does not abandon your earned lap progress.
Principal jump: smooth 40m takeoff with 6.2m rise; tested approach speeds about129-150km/h.
The ordinary road bypass and valid alternative routes remain available.
Small vehicles remain vulnerable to cars. They do not gain hidden speed or launch boosts.

New record categories separate these recovery/jump/roster rules from old records.
Old records and saved volumes/settings remain preserved. Tests use isolated save folders.
Virtual-input tests do not establish physical-controller coverage or Dan's acceptance.
Traffic contacts and AI recovery can still cost time; subjective Hard difficulty needs review.
No multiplayer, online services or paid vehicle assets. Nothing uploaded/distributed.

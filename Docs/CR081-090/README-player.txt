CR-081 + CR-090 combined review build

Launch Racer.exe beside this file. The complete runtime is required.
Project shortcut: C:\Users\danmo\Racer\Builds\Latest\Racer.exe

Speed traps and authored jumps report automatically in races and free roam.
Drive through a trap; land cleanly after a marked jump. Results appear at the
lower left with mph/feet, medal and PB. Reset, teleport, wet/hard/unstable
landings cannot earn a valid jump. Activities do not grant race checkpoints.

ACTIVITY RECORDS: main menu, pause menu or results. Choose Speed Traps or
Jumps, then a site. Current vehicle/layout/direction categories are separate.
Browse saved/historical categories to see older PBs and attempts. Old bests
have an unknown date, not an invented date. Tied attempts remain distinct.
The existing lap and total-race boards remain available separately.

GHOSTS: Exploration / personal best, from the main menu, pause or results.
Toggle Clean-lap ghost ON. Complete a clean lap to create a local pose ghost.
No resets, teleports or missed gates; authorized shortcuts qualify. Only a
faster compatible clean lap replaces it. Select the same course, direction
and vehicle to replay. Historical incompatible files are retained. This
stricter ghost category does not change the broader race/lap record rules.
Race with opponents off for solo time-trial practice. Ghosts never collide.

EXPLORATION: enter Free Roam. Find 24 golden woodland acorns: 12 around the
neighborhood roads and 12 on the mountain trails. Progress appears in the
lower-left HUD and Exploration / personal best. Discoveries persist across
tracks and relaunch. There are no currency rewards, upgrades or race locks.

Follow Kyle's wooded driveway opposite the Dan/House 2 fence transition.
Continue around either shore of the lake to the adjoining wooded mountain.
Creek ascent climbs toward Fern Creek Leap and the ridge. The other side
returns through the northern saddle and laurel descent. Both trails connect
back to the lake. Approach challenging jumps centered and at the posted
speed; exploratory opposite/edge approaches can be unstable (see report).
Trickum is the western connecting road. Jamerson is the separate road at
South Cherokee Lane's southern end; these names are not interchangeable.

CONTROLS: D-pad/stick or arrows to select; A/Space to confirm; B/Esc back.
Start/Esc pauses/resumes. Existing driving and radio controls are retained.
Track-menu changes should preserve the current song. Music subfolders are
channels; nested folders remain part of their parent channel. See RADIO.md.

Trickum Rd and S Cherokee Ln roadside (Woodstock Rush 0.113)

The placement of the houses, the church, the meeting hall, the drives, parking lots, side streets and fences along Trickum Rd and the west end of S Cherokee Ln follows the real roads today, taken from:

- OpenStreetMap: map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0,
  https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- USGS National Agriculture Imagery Program (NAIP) aerial imagery via The National Map (U.S. Geological Survey), public domain.

Neither is shown in the game; they were used for placement only. Every subdivision, church and hall name in the game is invented; no real names, symbols or trade dress are used.
